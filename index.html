The ZerøMercy track back to the early days of the underground scene. That was when the first foundation dropped, created by innovators who lived for the next release. It was a powerhouse, pure and simple, pulling in active supporters across the network.

By early 2021, the community went ghost, and for a long time, the name was just a memory. Fast forward to March 19, 2023. The brand was pulled out of the graveyard and published again. This era was different, grittier. It was all about exploitation, hacks, and tools.

That was the day the name became what it is now, ZerøMercy. It stopped being just about tools and became a roster of the heavy hitters, the fearless, and most famous members across the communities. It is active, loud, and stacked with talent.
